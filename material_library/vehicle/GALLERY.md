# Vehicle material preview gallery

Representative procedural family recipes rendered by the mtlx CLI on an Apple M3 hardware GPU.
Each sheet shows a 1 m plane, 20 cm close-up, 5 cm detail, sphere, and totem under neutral lighting.
The plane views are metric. Sphere and totem UVs are illustrative.

46 representatives from all 31 catalog families. See [QA.md](QA.md) for coverage and limits.
